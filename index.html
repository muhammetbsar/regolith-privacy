Regolith: Lunar Colony – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This policy applies to the Android game Regolith: Lunar Colony (package com.Regolith), published by Regolith Games ("we", "us").

What we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. The game has no user accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and it does not ask for any device permission.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (resources, gear, missions) and your settings (volume, graphics quality) are saved locally on your device only. Uninstalling the game removes them.

Third parties

We do not share data with third parties. Google Play may process standard information about your installation under Google's own privacy policy.

Children

The game is rated for ages 13 and over and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated date.

Contact

Questions: regolithcolony@gmail.com
